Digital Projects

A selection of digital banking projects where I contributed to translating business needs into practical solutions through cross-functional collaboration.

01 / DIGITAL BANKING

Mobile Banking App Update

Ziraat Bank Azerbaijan

Project overview

An update of the mobile banking application, focusing on the presentation of card information, navigation, and access to banking services.

My contribution

Collaborated with the IT team, collected and clarified business requirements, helped define user stories, participated in testing, and provided feedback on improvements.

Customer value

Improved access to card information and a more convenient mobile banking experience.

02 / DIGITAL BANKING

SWIFT GPI — International Transfer Tracking

Ziraat Bank Azerbaijan

Project overview

Introduction of SWIFT GPI tracking functionality through Internet Banking, enabling customers to follow the status of international transfers.

My contribution

Prepared technical and functional requirements, coordinated with banking operations specialists, and collaborated with the frontend development team on the functionality and user interface.

Customer value

Greater transparency and the ability to track international transfers independently.
