WHAT I'VE DONE / SOCIAL PROJECTS

Social Projects

A selection of initiatives combining social responsibility, sustainability, education, and community engagement.

01 / TEAM BUILDING & ENVIRONMENT

Growing Together — Tree Planting Initiative

Expressbank | 2018

Project overview

A team-building initiative combining employee engagement with environmental responsibility. The event brought together 60 head-office employees to plant trees and contribute to a greener environment.

My contribution

Initiated the project idea and collaborated with colleagues to organize the tree-planting event, including lunch arrangements. Later, represented the initiative at an environmental forum, sharing our experience with participants.

Project outcome

The event strengthened team connections while contributing to environmental protection. An agreement was also reached to develop an irrigation system for the planted trees, supporting their long-term growth.

02 / FINANCIAL LITERACY

Future Bankers — Financial Literacy for Children

Ziraat Bank Azerbaijan

Project overview

Educational initiative designed to introduce children to banking, financial services, and different professional roles through practical experience.

My contribution

Initiated the project idea and helped organize an interactive visit to the bank's head office. Prepared a presentation, guided the children during the visit, and coordinated with employees who introduced their departments and explained how bank cards and ATMs work.

Project outcome

Children gained practical exposure to banking operations and financial concepts through interactive learning activities.

03 / ENVIRONMENTAL EDUCATION

Environmental Education Tour for Children

Ziraat Bank Azerbaijan

Project overview

An educational visit to Təmiz Şəhər facilities ahead of COP29, introducing children to waste management, recycling, and environmental protection.

My contribution

Collaborated with the team to plan and organize the educational event, coordinate communication activities, and promote the initiative to raise awareness of environmental responsibility.

Project outcome

Children had the opportunity to observe waste sorting and recycling processes firsthand and gain practical knowledge about environmental protection.

04 / SUSTAINABILITY

Green Office — Paper Recycling Initiative

Ziraat Bank Azerbaijan

Project overview

A corporate sustainability initiative focused on collecting used paper across the bank's head office and branches and transferring it to a specialized recycling company.

My contribution

Negotiated with the recycling company and developed and coordinated the paper collection and transfer process, including collection points, transportation procedures, and the secure handling of confidential documents. Established and obtained approval for procedures to ensure consistent implementation across the bank.

Project outcome

More than 400 kg of paper was collected and transferred for recycling within the first six months, supporting responsible resource management and environmental sustainability.
